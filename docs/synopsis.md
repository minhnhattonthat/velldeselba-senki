# Synopsis (for translators; full of spoilers)

Built 2026-10-23 from five reader passes over all dialogue batches (work/syn notes). Story order is inferred from
crew chatter (I01_00) and mission numbering; file order is NOT story order. "Unclear" means unclear.

## World
- Velldeselba is split north/south by mountains airships can't cross. North: the **Munogi Federal Republic** ("the
  Federation"). South: the **Kingdom of Glacial Gidan** ("Gidan", capital Glacial). Between them the **Kuna Republic**
  (a Federation vassal). Centre: **Oldanaspi**, permanently neutral "navel of Velldeselba" (capital Danaspi, king
  Danaspi IV, descendant of the hero Da Danaspi who won independence from the Pask ~400 years ago; holy land Selva).
  North-west: the **Noipask Republic**.
- The **Pask** fell from the sky long ago, mined the planet, and lifted land with anti-gravity engines into **floating
  islands**. The Noipask are their descendants; they despise the planet's people and are disliked in turn. Their old
  Pask machinery is failing (an "island drop" massacre was an accident of a failing engine; Sidel calls it a crime).
- Airships are built from a very light wood. Sky pirates (Da Kuda of Dak, Buabnam of Daha) raid only military
  convoys and redistribute goods to "keep the balance" so no nation grows too strong.

## The player
Fell from space (default name Misaki, male, rough and casual, money-minded but principled). Taken in by Noipask on
Nades Island, a Noipask floating island inside Gidan's borders. He wants to build a ship to go home.

## Story by phase
1. **Noipask (G10-G12 maps; G00-G07, G41-G46 missions).** Colonel Zexel makes him a Second Lieutenant over Commander
   Odenaude's contempt; Lieutenant Colonel Kafi (woman) is his ally; crew Oyu Kikuru, Hauezna, later Ba Magg, Ich,
   Nich. Gidan raids Nades; the homeland orders a retreat to the Federation city Miderekisata. Kafi and the player
   plan it with Federation Captain De Zeshika (daughter of General De Saro) and the twin commanders Kosterif and
   Takaterif. The plan leaks; Gidan ambushes the Federation force; Zexel fakes moving the island, abandons it and
   breaks through. Da Kuda boards, says he also "fell from space", and asks the player to join the pirates
   (yes/no: the route split, see below).
2. **Danaspi crisis (D01, D02, C03).** Odenaude and Zexel occupy neutral Oldanaspi on homeland orders and confine
   Danaspi IV; Zexel jails Ag (youngest of the Federation's Invincible Three Brothers), the Federation delegation and
   Kafi, who opposed it. The Noipask-Federation alliance breaks. The player is ordered to hold Denamus; he breaks with
   Noipask, frees the prisoners (Zexel escapes). Ag joins with the king's blessing.
3. **Route A, soldier (G missions).** Gidan Colonel Gashizakino, secretly working for peace with Kafi, hires the
   player as a mercenary at Slark (Second Lieutenant, later Captain). Crew gains Sergeant Ka Jarko and Sergeant Major
   Fragnum (women), Karetz and Boi. Gidan takes Denamus, Danaspi, Kuna City, the Noipask islands I-Dem, Ni-Dem, Mi-Dem;
   the Noipask homeland falls. **Zexel dies in a last stand** (reported in G05). Admiral **Sidel**, who holds real
   power, keeps conquering. The player meets the **King of Gidan** in Glacial and talks him into ceasefire talks; on
   the way to Kuna City Sidel's Royal Guard under Bams shoots the royal ship down. The player and the King hide on
   merchants Pack and Gould's ship at Gajis. The King defects to the Federation; the player joins the Federation army
   (Captain) under Colonel Mahashi at Yadas; Bams drops Hounom Island on Ferum; Commander **Takaterif is killed**;
   Youtakon, Ayamemi, Panasgal retaken; Gidan royalist Kakanan joins.
4. **Route B, sky pirate (K missions; A, B, E maps).** The player flies with Da Kuda and Buabnam (adviser Mardaman).
   He rescues a kidnapped old man who turns out to be the **King of Gidan** (Stiltog Ba Dusk de Gidan; aide Garks);
   the kidnapper was one of the King's own aides, Sidel behind it. Kafi, suspected of being a Noipask spy, is handed
   over by Gashizakino as a "prisoner" (with Ka Jarko and Fragnum); Karf, Kafi's twin sister and a pirate dancer,
   joins. Royalist uprising at the Adakuda prison camp (Kamchom freed, Kenri's father). Zeshika now hunts pirates but
   lets them go; Federation soldier Makirov turns pirate. Women pirates Illumina, Defaina, Silka and drunk captain
   Chibaraga join.
5. **Finale (both routes: A12-A13, B04, H01; G37-G40/G52/G55, K24/K25/K28/K64).** Sidel reveals himself as the
   **Arbiter**: with Miyuki (another girl who fell from space, his ward) he broadcasts telepathically to the whole
   planet: lay down arms, give up nations and peoples. He builds a mechanised floating island / aerial fortress
   ("Totem Pole") and heads for Danaspi. All factions unite (Kafi, Zeshika, Gashizakino, Da Kuda, Kakanan, the King).
   Through Houdepui Desert, Ogatsu, Madels Gorge and Dant to the fortress. **The King calls Sidel "my son";** Sidel
   accuses him of not believing in people, even his own son, sends Miyuki away with the player, and **dies**.
6. **Epilogue (H10).** One year later: a mock-battle ceremony, character bios, bonus mode. Miyuki is treated as the
   King's granddaughter; Kafi helps Noipask civilians resettle and hints she'd ride his spaceship home with him.
   H10 text writes ミサキ directly (not `<F62>`): translate as "Misaki".

## Reveals to keep hidden early
- Sidel is the King's son (some NPCs say it openly mid-game: translate them as written, but don't hint before).
- Kafi grew up in Gidan; her parents (father an ex-Noipask ambassador) live outside Yazusa.
- Zexel's betrayal of the alliance; Zexel's death; Takaterif's death; Bams's assassination attempt.
- The kidnapped "old man" is the King.

## Relationships
- Kafi and Karf: twins (Kafi elder); Karf calls her "Sis". Their father is stern and estranged.
- Gashizakino and Kafi: secret peace partners. Gashizakino and Da Kuda / Buabnam: allies late.
- Zeshika: proud, fond of the player; pursued by Rauel (trading-company president) and Makirov; her father wants her
  married. President Otawa tries to match her with the player.
- Oyu Kikuru adores Kafi and has a crush on the player. Hauezna marries the nurse Meer (Ku Meer). Ka Jarko is
  pursued by Second Lieutenant Milt. Ag calls the player "big bro" (兄貴). Pack calls him "boss" (だんな).
- Mahashi, Kachita, Ag: the Invincible Three Brothers (Federation).

## Voices (beyond docs/style-reference.md)
- King of Gidan: archaic royal (余, じゃ, そなた), jolly gourmand, "wahaha"; the player calls him "gramps".
- Sidel: every line opens with "...", slow, messianic; anguished at the end.
- Da Kuda: booming, folksy, drinks (がはは). Buabnam: loud drunk. Chibaraga: hiccuping drunk ("Hic!").
- Gashizakino: hearty "gahaha", formal on duty. Bams: haughty "-tamae" officer. Kazushitaka: apoplectic stammer.
- Kosterif/Takaterif: genteel old twins, "hohoho", finish each other's sentences (keep the split).
- Fragnum: refined lady (わたくし, ですわ). Ka Jarko: cheerful, nervous tomboy. Ba Magg, Ich, Nich: over-formal
  soldier speech (であります), slightly comic. Danaspi IV: rambling elderly sage. Abunas: geeky stammerer.
- Defaina: "ohoho" vamp; Silka: giggly; Illumina: tough leader. Rauel: melodramatic; his secretary ultra-formal.
- Danadeuno old folk tag lines with "...tasty, eh?" (うまかろ). H10 has parodies (a Super Saiyan gag, a "love and
  peace" song): keep the gag.
